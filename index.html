Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum investment amount?

Our minimum investment starts at £1,000 for most funds. Premium tier investments require £25,000 minimum. VIP exclusive opportunities are available from £100,000. All investments can be made using multiple payment methods including bank transfer, cryptocurrency, PayPal, and gift cards.

How quickly can I withdraw my funds?

Standard withdrawals are processed within 24-48 hours. Express withdrawals (additional 0.5% fee) are processed within 2 hours. Large withdrawals over £50,000 may require additional security verification taking up to 72 hours. All withdrawals show "Pending" status until processed.

What security measures protect my account?

We implement military-grade security including: 256-bit SSL encryption, mandatory 2FA authentication, biometric login options, real-time fraud monitoring, £250M insurance coverage, and 98% cold storage for digital assets. Our platform is SOC 2 Type II certified and regulated by the FCA.

Can I access my investments from mobile?

Yes! Our mobile app is available for iOS and Android with full functionality. Track portfolios, execute trades, deposit/withdraw funds, and access customer support 24/7. The app features biometric authentication and real-time push notifications.

What fees does Apex Prime charge?

We charge a competitive 1.5% annual management fee on most funds. Performance fees of 15% apply only when returns exceed 15% annually. There are no deposit fees, and withdrawal fees range from 0% to 0.5% depending on method. VIP members enjoy reduced fees.
